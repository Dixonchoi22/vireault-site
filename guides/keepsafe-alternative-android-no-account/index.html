Keepsafe alternative without an account (Android)

Keepsafe is one of the best-known Android photo vaults — Google Play lists it at50M+ downloads with ads and in-app purchases on the free tier (Play listing, checked Nov 2026). Many people who search for a “Keepsafe alternative” are not asking for another cloud gallery. They want a vault that works without creating an email account, without ads, and with encryption details they can read.

This page stays factual. It cites Keepsafe’s own help center and Play listing, notes what a third-party comparison observed about ads, and explains where Vireault fits — including what Vireault does not ship yet. It is not a claim that Keepsafe is unsafe for every user.

What Keepsafe’s public docs say about accounts and recovery

  • Keepsafe’s help articleHow does Keepsafe protect my data?describes 256-bit AES encryption at rest, Private Cloud backups, and PIN/pattern unlock. For a forgotten PIN it points to a reset process that uses anaccess code through your account email.
  • The same article states that Basic (free) backs up the first 200 items; Premium raises the cloud cap (Keepsafe documents up to 10,000). It also warns that losing a device without backup means photos and videos are gone forever.
  • A separate help article states Keepsafe isnot currently designed as a completely zero-knowledge app, and lists limited cases where the company can view or share personal data (user-requested support, legal requirements, Terms/Privacy concerns):Can Keepsafe see any of my content?.
  • Trash recovery is documented as a Premium feature:Recovering items from Trash.

None of that means Keepsafe “does not encrypt.” It means the product model includes an account, a vendor cloud with free-tier caps, and a trust boundary that is not zero-knowledge by Keepsafe’s own wording. If that is what you want to avoid, look for a local vault.

What “no account” actually buys you

A no-account Android vault typically means:

  • No email or phone number required to open or unlock the vault
  • No vendor login that can reset access to your ciphertext
  • Keys derived on-device from a secret only you hold
  • Backups as files you copy (USB, Files, your own Drive), not an automatic vendor cloud

The tradeoff is blunt: if you forget the secret and lose the backup recovery material,nobody can restore the files — including the developer. That is the cost of not giving a company a recovery path. Read that before migrating irreplaceable media.

Checklist: Keepsafe alternative without an account

  1. Signup. Does the app insist on an email before you can store files?
  2. Crypto detail. Does it name a KDF and AEAD (for example Argon2id + AES-256-GCM), or only “military-grade”? Keepsafe’s Play listing uses the latter phrasing without a cipher mode on the store page.
  3. Ads. York Vault’s Oct 2026 Android comparison of seven mainstream vaults (Keepsafe, NQ Vault, Hide It Pro, Calculator vault, Vaulty, LockMyPix, Private Photo Vault) reported that all seven Play listings said “Contains ads” on the free version (yorkvault.com/guides/best-photo-vault-apps-android). If you want a vault free of ad networks, filter for that explicitly.
  4. Backup you control. Prefer an encrypted export you can restore after uninstall — and practice one restore before deleting gallery originals. Seephoto vault lost my photos.
  5. Paywalls in front of your files. Check whether Trash recovery, basic unlock or export of your items requires Premium. Prefer products that never hold your ciphertext hostage.
  6. Honest missing features. A good alternative page admits what is not built yet instead of promising a full Keepsafe clone on day one.

Who might still prefer Keepsafe

  • You want vendor Private Cloud sync and are fine creating an account
  • You already rely on Keepsafe’s workflow, Safe Send or multi-device habits
  • You need mature Premium extras (album locks, break-in alerts, fake PIN) that Vireault has not shipped
  • You value a long public review history over a newer indie vault
