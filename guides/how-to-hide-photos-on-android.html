How to hide private photos on Android (the right way)

Short answer: Most "hide" features just move a photo out of the gallery. The file is still readable by anyone who has your unlocked phone, a file manager, or a backup. To really protect a photo, encrypt it with a key that only you control, then delete the original.

Option 1: The gallery's "Hide" or "Archive" button

Google Photos Archive only takes photos out of the main grid. Anyone who opens the app can still see them, and they still sync to your Google account. Use it to tidy up, not to keep things private.

Option 2: Google Photos Locked Folder

Locked Folder keeps photos out of your grid and away from other apps, which is a real step up. The catch is that it uses the same PIN as your phone, so anyone who knows your screen lock can open it. Backup is optional: with it off, items exist only on that phone. See is Google Photos Locked Folder safe?

Option 3: Samsung Secure Folder / Private Space

Samsung's Secure Folder and Android 15's Private Space create a separate, encrypted profile. They're solid options, but not every phone has them, and they're heavy if all you want is to protect a few photos.

Option 4: An encrypted vault app

A good vault encrypts every file on your device with a strong cipher (such as AES-256), using a key derived from a PIN or pattern that's separate from your phone unlock. Before you trust one, check that:

  • files are actually encrypted, not just renamed or hidden in a ".nomedia" folder
  • there's no account or server that holds your key
  • it blocks screenshots and locks when you leave the app

Step by step: moving photos into a vault

  1. Install the vault and set a PIN or pattern that's different from your screen lock.
  2. Tap Add photos / files and choose your photos.
  3. Once they're imported, delete the originals from the gallery and from Google Photos' Trash.
  4. If you use cloud backup, also remove the copies that were already uploaded.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting the cloud copy. Deleting a photo on your phone doesn't always delete it from the cloud.
  • Reusing your screen-lock PIN.
  • Treating the vault as a backup. Vireault files stay on this phone only, so a lost phone means lost files unless you keep another copy somewhere safe.
  • Expecting a reset. With true zero-knowledge encryption, a forgotten PIN means the files are gone for good. Write it down somewhere safe.
