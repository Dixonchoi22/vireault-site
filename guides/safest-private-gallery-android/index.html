Safest private gallery app for Android

No reviewer can crown a single forever-winner. Threat models differ. What you can do is score apps against a checklist that weeds out disguise-only tools and vague “military grade” claims.

Buyer’s checklist

  1. Are algorithms named (for example AES-256-GCM and a modern KDF)?
  2. Is an account required to store files?
  3. Does the privacy policy say vault contents are not collected?
  4. Can you export an encrypted backup you control?
  5. Is there lockout / auto-lock behavior?
  6. Can you delete originals after import?
  7. Are missing features admitted in public docs?

How Vireault scores on that list

Vireault documents Argon2id, AES-256-GCM, a random master key and an encrypted index. It is offline and local only, with no account. It supports PIN, 3×3 or 5×5 pattern or passphrase, optional fingerprint unlock, lockout, auto-lock, screenshot blocking, media/PDF import, folders and notes, in-app capture, search/sort/filter, optional original deletion, and encrypted backup/restore.

Apps to compare, not dismiss

Older incumbents still help many people. Read our fair notes on Keepsafe, Hide it Pro and calculator vault apps before you migrate a large library.

What “safest” cannot mean

It cannot mean invulnerable. A weak passphrase, malware with accessibility abuse, or a compromised unlocked session can defeat almost any gallery app. Safety is layered: device screen lock, vault encryption, backups, and cautious sharing.
