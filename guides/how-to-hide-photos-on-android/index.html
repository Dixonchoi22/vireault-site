How to hide photos on Android

People search for “hide photos on Android” when a shared phone, a borrowed device, or a nosy gallery scroll becomes a problem. Android does not give every manufacturer the same privacy tools, so the honest answer is a short menu of options with different strengths.

1. Use your gallery’s built-in hide or Locked Folder

Google Photos offers a Locked Folder on many devices. Some OEM galleries also let you move albums out of the main grid. These tools are convenient and free, but they usually stay inside one vendor ecosystem and may still leave originals elsewhere if you share or sync aggressively.

2. Move files out of DCIM without encryption

Copying images into an obscure folder or prefixing names with a dot is a weak form of hiding. Anyone who can browse storage, plug the phone into a computer, or open a file manager can still find them. Treat this as temporary tidying, not protection.

3. Use an encrypted vault app

4. Checklist before you call a photo “hidden”

  1. Is the file encrypted, or only removed from the gallery UI?
  2. Did cloud backup already upload a copy?
  3. Did messaging apps keep a compressed duplicate?
  4. Do you have an encrypted backup if the phone dies?
  5. Is unlock protected with lockout, not a trivial PIN?
