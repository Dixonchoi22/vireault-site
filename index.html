How it works

From install to locked vault in about a minute.

  1. 1

    Choose how you unlock

    Set a PIN, draw a 3×3 or 5×5 pattern, or type a passphrase. Argon2id protects the key that unlocks a random vault master key. Compatible devices can enable fingerprint unlock after an unlock with your secret.

  2. 2

    Bring in your media

    Pick photos, videos, audio or PDFs. Each one is encrypted as it enters the vault, and you can remove the original from your gallery afterwards.

  3. 3

    Close it and it locks

    Switch apps or leave the phone idle for five minutes and the vault seals itself. Screenshots of vault screens are blocked.

The security model, in plain words

  • Your secret is never stored. Argon2id turns it into a key each time you unlock.
  • A random master key encrypts file contents with AES-256-GCM, which also detects tampering.
  • The index is encrypted too, so file names and thumbnails are unreadable on disk.
  • Repeated wrong attempts trigger a lockout delay.
  • Backups are exported as encrypted files protected by their own recovery material.

Frequently asked questions

Can Vireault reset my PIN if I forget it?

We cannot reset it for you. If you set up and retain your user-held 12-word recovery phrase, you can use it to choose a new unlock secret. Skipping setup means a forgotten secret can permanently lock you out. Separate encrypted backups need their own recovery material.

Does it upload anything?

The app does not upload vault content to us and has no built-in cloud sync. You can deliberately export files or encrypted backups to a destination you choose. Purchases use Google Play Billing.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free covers 1 vault and 50 items. Pro is $1.99/week, $19.99/year with a 3-day free trial, or $29.99 once for lifetime.

Can I move my vault to a new phone?

Export an encrypted backup, transfer it yourself, and restore it using that backup's recovery material. Keep that material separately and safely.

Does it have folders, fingerprint unlock or a decoy vault?

The current build supports folders, encrypted notes, compatible-device fingerprint unlock, in-app photo/video capture, and Pro hidden-list / decoy use. Cloud sync is not built in. Hidden mode is not forensic-proof.
