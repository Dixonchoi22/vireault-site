A private vault for your photos and files. Encrypted on your phone.

Vireault encrypts everything with AES-256 on your device, using a key that only your PIN or pattern can unlock. We run no servers and have no copy of your key, so we can't open your vault. Nobody can force us to, because we couldn't if we tried.

Get it on Google Play – free

Features

  • Zero-knowledge encryption. AES-256-GCM, with your key protected by Argon2id. It's derived from your PIN or pattern and never leaves your phone.
  • PIN, pattern or passphrase. Change it whenever you like. The vault locks itself as soon as you leave the app, or after 5 minutes without a touch, and wipes its key from memory.
  • No accounts and no cloud. There's nothing to hack on our side.
  • Screenshot protection. Vault screens don't appear in screenshots or the recent-apps view.
  • Clear out the originals. After importing, delete the originals from your phone. Each encrypted copy is checked first.
  • Search, sort and thumbnails. Find files by name or type, and select several at once. Thumbnails are encrypted too.
  • Videos, audio and PDFs open in the app. They play and display without a decrypted copy being saved to storage. Swipe between items and pinch to zoom photos.
  • Encrypted backups. Save your vault as one encrypted file, checked after saving, and restore it on a new phone with a code only you have.
  • Free to start. 1 vault and 50 photos or files, with the same encryption as Pro.
  • Pro: unlimited vaults and items, from $1.99 a week. See pricing.

Before you start

There's no reset. If you forget your PIN or pattern, nobody can recover your files, including us. Vaults stay on your phone and nothing is backed up automatically, so make an encrypted backup and keep it, and its code, somewhere safe. How Vireault protects your files, and where the limits are.
