Common questions

What happens to my files if Pro ends?

Everything already in your vaults stays yours and stays readable. You just can't add more past the free limits.

How do I cancel?

Weekly and Annual renew automatically until you cancel in Google Play. Lifetime has nothing to cancel.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases. This restores Pro, not your files: uninstalling the app erases your vaults, because they're stored only on your phone. To keep your files, make an encrypted backup first.

Is backup a Pro feature?

No. Encrypted backup and restore are on every plan, and restoring a backup is never limited by the free plan's item count. Your files are yours.

Does paying make my vault more secure?

No. Free and Pro use the same encryption. Read how Vireault's encryption works.
