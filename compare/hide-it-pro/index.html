Choosing between them

If you already know Hide it Pro’s interface and it meets your needs, switching is optional. Choose Vireault if you specifically want a no-account vault that documents Argon2id, AES-256-GCM, encrypted backups and optional original deletion after import.

Limits to remember

Vireault now includes folders, fingerprint unlock on compatible devices, in-app camera capture, and Pro hidden/decoy vaults. It still has no built-in cloud sync. Hide it Pro may include workflows outside that list; verify on Google Play before you decide.
