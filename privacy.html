Privacy policy

Vireault collects no personal data. Your vault is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your PIN or pattern, and it never leaves the device unless you choose to save a copy of a file or an encrypted backup outside the vault. We never receive those copies. We run no servers that store your files or keys. Purchases are handled by Google Play. If you forget your PIN, we can't recover your data.
