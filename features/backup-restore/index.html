Encrypted backup and restore

Local-only storage still needs an escape hatch when you change phones. Vireault lets you export an encrypted backup file and restore it later.

Backup basics

  • Export an encrypted backup file you control
  • Restore on a new device with your recovery material
  • Keep recovery codes offline and private

If you forget your unlock secret and lose recovery material, nobody can reconstruct the vault — including us. That is the cost of not holding keys on a server.
