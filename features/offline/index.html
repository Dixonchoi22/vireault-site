Offline and local only

Some vault apps pair convenience with accounts and sync. Vireault takes the opposite default: your encrypted library stays on the phone you hold.

No account

There is no email signup and no phone-number login. You create a vault, choose an unlock method, and start importing.

No cloud vault server

We do not host your photos or files. If you want a copy elsewhere, you export an encrypted backup file and store it where you choose.

Use a vault that works without an account

Encrypt photos, videos and files on your phone. Offline and local only — no account required.

Trade-offs we will not hide

  • Losing the device without a backup can mean losing access to the vault data
  • Cloud sync is not built in
  • We cannot reset a forgotten PIN, pattern or passphrase

Those trade-offs are the point of a local-first design. Compare approaches in Vireault vs Keepsafe, the Keepsafe alternative without an account guide, and secure photo vault for Android.
