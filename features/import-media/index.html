Import and view private media

Vireault is for more than still photos. Bring in the files you actually need to keep private, then browse them without dumping decrypted copies back into the gallery.

Supported today

  • Photos with swipe and zoom
  • Video and audio with scrubbing
  • PDF viewing inside the vault
  • Search, sort and filter by kind
  • Optional delete of the original after import

Folders, notes and in-app capture

Items can live in folders, and you can write encrypted notes. The in-app camera saves photos into encrypted vault storage rather than the gallery. Video recording uses a temporary private spool that is discarded if you leave the app or lock the vault before the recording finishes.
