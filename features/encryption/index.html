AES-256 encryption on your Android phone

Vireault is built around on-device cryptography. Content is not “hidden” by renaming a folder or changing an icon. Files are encrypted before they rest in the vault.

What ships today

  • Argon2id key derivation from your unlock secret
  • AES-256-GCM for vault file contents
  • A random master key that wraps the encrypted data
  • An encrypted index so file names stay hidden on disk

Why the index matters

Even when file bytes are encrypted, cleartext names can leak what you stored. Vireault encrypts the index as well, which reduces that metadata leak on the device filesystem.

What encryption does not replace

If someone knows your PIN, pattern or passphrase, they can open the vault. Choose a strong secret, enable lockout discipline, and keep backup recovery material offline. Related reading: unlock methods, offline design, encrypting files on Android, and Keepsafe alternative without an account.

Honest boundaries

We do not claim third-party audits on this page. We describe the algorithms and local model the app uses. For the plain-language model — including what encryption does not protect against — see the security page. For privacy practices, see the privacy policy.
