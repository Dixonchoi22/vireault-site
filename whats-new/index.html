What's new

Release notes and what we are working on next.

Up next

Cloud sync is not planned as a built-in feature. Remaining work is polish, release review and listing the app — not those already-shipped local features.

Releases

Development build — 7 November 2026

  • 5×5 pattern unlock, folders and encrypted notes.
  • Compatible-device fingerprint unlock (first unlock after reboot still needs your secret).
  • User-held 12-word recovery phrase, with an informed skip.
  • In-app photo and video capture into the vault. Video uses a temporary private spool while recording; unfinished recordings are discarded on lock or background.
  • Pro hidden-list / decoy vaults and alternate app icons. Hidden mode is not forensic-proof.

Earlier

First release — November 2026

  • Vaults encrypted with Argon2id + AES-256-GCM, including an encrypted index.
  • PIN, 3×3 or 5×5 pattern or passphrase unlock with lockout after wrong attempts.
  • Multiple vaults, each with its own secret.
  • Import and view photos, video, audio and PDF, with search, sort and filters.
  • Encrypted backup and restore, auto-lock and screenshot blocking.
  • Free plan (1 vault, 50 items) and Pro: $1.99/week, $19.99/year with a 3-day free trial, or $29.99 lifetime.
