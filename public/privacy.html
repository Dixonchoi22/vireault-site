Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Vireault collects no personal data. Your vault lives only on your device, and nothing in it is sent to us or anyone else.

Your unlock secret

You unlock your vault with a PIN, a pattern or a passphrase. It is used on your device to derive the encryption key and is never sent anywhere. We can't see it, reset it or recover it.

Recovery phrase

You can choose to get a 12-word recovery phrase. It lets you set a new unlock secret. Only you hold it. We can't recover it, and we can't reset a vault by email. If you skipped the phrase and forget your unlock secret, the vault can't be opened, unless you have your own separate encrypted backup and its recovery material.

Encryption

Keys are derived with Argon2id, and files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. All of this happens on your device.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you take a photo or video inside the app. Captures go straight into the encrypted vault, not your phone's gallery. The microphone is used only to record sound for videos you record in the app. Nothing leaves your device.

Biometrics

Fingerprint or face unlock is optional and uses Android's BiometricPrompt. Android handles your biometric data. It never reaches the app or us.

What the app doesn't do

The app has no analytics, no ads, no crash-reporting SDK and no accounts. The app's own code doesn't send anything over the internet. The Google Play Billing library it uses includes internet access so Google Play can process purchases.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase go through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and purchase data under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive your card details.

Deleting your data

Your data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or deleting a vault removes it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email on Vireault's Google Play listing.
