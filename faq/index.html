Frequently asked questions

Straight answers based on what the Android app supports today.

Does Vireault need an account?

No. There is no signup, email or phone number. The vault lives on your device.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Vireault is offline and local only. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing; the app does not send your vault contents to us.

What encryption does Vireault use?

Argon2id for key derivation, AES-256-GCM for file contents, a random master key, and an encrypted index so file names stay hidden.

How do I unlock the vault?

Choose a PIN, a 3×3 or 5×5 pattern, or a passphrase. Compatible devices can enable fingerprint unlock after unlocking with your secret. The first unlock after reboot requires your secret. Wrong unlock attempts trigger lockout delays.

Can I have more than one vault?

Yes. Several vaults are supported. The free plan includes 1 vault and 50 items; Pro raises those limits.

What file types can I import?

Photos, video, audio and PDF. You can view them inside the vault and optionally delete the original after import.

Does Vireault auto-lock?

Yes. It locks when the app goes to the background or after about 5 minutes idle. Screenshots are blocked.

How do backups work?

You export an encrypted backup file and restore it later. Keep your recovery material safe; if you lose your secret and recovery data, the files cannot be recovered.

Do you have cloud sync, biometrics, decoy vaults or a camera?

The current build supports folders, encrypted notes, compatible-device fingerprint unlock, in-app photo/video capture, and Pro hidden-list / decoy use. Cloud sync is not built in. Hidden mode is not forensic-proof.

What is the 12-word recovery phrase?

Keep the user-held 12-word phrase offline and private. It allows you to reset the vault unlock secret. Skipping it requires explicit acknowledgement that a forgotten unlock secret may permanently lose access; we have no email reset.

Does the in-app camera save to my gallery?

Photo capture saves to encrypted vault storage rather than the gallery. During video recording only, the app needs an unencrypted spool in private app storage. An unfinished recording is discarded and its spool deleted when you leave the app or lock the vault. Power-loss / force-kill residue was not tested.

Can I export files on the free plan?

Export is free and can preserve folders. Free includes one vault and 50 items; Pro adds unlimited limits and privacy conveniences, not stronger cryptography.

How much does Pro cost?

Pro is $1.99/week, $19.99/year with a 3-day free trial, or $29.99 lifetime via Google Play.
