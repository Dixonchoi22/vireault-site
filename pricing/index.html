What Pro is for

The free plan is enough to evaluate encryption, unlock methods, import, viewing, search and backup. Pro raises capacity so you can keep more vaults and items as your library grows.

What we do not claim

  • No built-in cloud sync
  • Fingerprint unlock is available on compatible devices after unlocking with your secret; it is not available in hidden-list mode
  • Hidden-list and decoy vaults are Pro features and are not forensic-proof
  • In-app camera saves into the vault; video uses a temporary private spool while recording
  • Folders and encrypted notes are included
