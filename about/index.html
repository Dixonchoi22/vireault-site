About Vireault

Vireault is a small, independent Android app with one job: keep the photos and files you choose encrypted on your own phone.

Why it exists

Many "hide" apps on Android simply move files into a folder the gallery skips, or put a calculator screen in front of them. The files themselves often stay readable to anyone who plugs the phone into a computer. Vireault was built to close that gap with real encryption rather than disguise.

What we believe

  • Your data, your device. No account and no cloud. We cannot see your vault, so we cannot lose it or hand it over.
  • Honest claims. The site lists features that ship today, including folders, fingerprint unlock, recovery phrases, in-app camera capture and Pro hidden / decoy vaults. Cloud sync is not built in, and hidden mode is not forensic-proof.
  • Fair pricing. A useful free tier, and a one-time lifetime option for people who dislike subscriptions.

How it is built

Keys are derived with Argon2id, files are sealed with AES-256-GCM, and the index is encrypted so names stay hidden. Read the feature pages for detail, or the privacy policy for exactly what is (and is not) collected.
